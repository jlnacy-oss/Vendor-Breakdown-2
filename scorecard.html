01

How the score works

Each vendor is scored out of 100 from its engagement threads — not from how good the vendor is. A high score means a broad, deep, live relationship; a low score means we've barely started. Breadth (30) distinct PortCos introduced, capped at five. Depth (30) the furthest status any thread has reached. Momentum (20) how recently anything moved. Substance (20) the share of threads with a real use case written down. A number in Score Override on the Vendors sheet replaces the calculation entirely — use it when the arithmetic misses something you know.
