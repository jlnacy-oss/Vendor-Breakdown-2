Today: Leads Alphabet overall and sets the capital envelope behind Google Cloud and the Gemini programme — 2026 capital expenditure guidance of $195–205B.

A product leader who became a platform CEO. He trained as a materials engineer at IIT Kharagpur, then took a master's at Stanford and an MBA at Wharton, working in engineering and product management at Applied Materials and then in management consulting at McKinsey. He joined Google in 2004 and led product management for its client software — Google Chrome and ChromeOS, and largely Google Drive — later overseeing Gmail and Google Maps, announcing the open-sourcing of the VP8 video codec and the WebM format in 2010, and taking on Android in 2013. He was made Product Chief, then selected as Google CEO in August 2015, stepping into the role that October when Alphabet was formed, and became Alphabet CEO in 2019.
