Company snapshot

Returned to what it calls the industry's winner's circle two years ahead of the target set at its Investor Day, on 7% revenue growth and 140 basis points of margin expansion. 28 large deals were signed in 2025 with total contract value up nearly 50% year over year.

Offering: Application services, digital engineering, cloud and infrastructure, and business process services. The Belcan acquisition added engineering, research and development capability aimed at a $190B market; Thirdera made it one of ServiceNow's largest partners with Global Elite status.

Where it sits: Cognizant's turnaround is real but it competes on the same ground as TCS, Infosys and Wipro, and its scale sits below Accenture and Capgemini. 05

Sentiment

Customer sentiment
Deal metrics are the strongest available proxy and they point the right way: large-deal total contract value up nearly 50%, two mega deals above $500M in Q4, and book-to-bill at roughly 1.3x. Cognizant publishes almost no attributable client outcomes, so the read rests on commercial signals rather than evidenced delivery.
Market & analyst sentiment
What supports the story
  • Growth restored to 7% with 140 basis points of margin expansion — both moving the right way at once
  • Book-to-bill of approximately 1.3x on $28.4B of trailing bookings is a healthy forward indicator
  • Belcan brings engineering R&D capability rather than more IT services headcount
  • Attrition improved to 13.9% from 15.9%, which matters for delivery continuity
What to pressure-test
  • 2026 guidance of 4.0–6.5% constant currency implies deceleration from 2025's 6.4%
  • Scale sits well below Accenture and Capgemini in a market where scale drives ecosystem access
  • Highly exposed to the same offshore labour-arbitrage model that AI delivery is compressing
  • Publishes essentially no attributable client outcomes, so capability claims rest on the firm's own framing
  • Margin guidance for 2026 of 15.9–16.1% implies only 10–30 basis points of further expansion
Employee sentiment
Headcount grew by 14,800 to 351,600 and voluntary attrition in tech services fell to 13.9% from 15.9% a year earlier — both point to a stabilising workforce after a turbulent period. Disclosed pay ratios are steep: the CEO's realised compensation was 76 times the median US employee and 237 times the global median, reflecting the India-weighted workforce.
